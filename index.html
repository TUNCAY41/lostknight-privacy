Lost Knight — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Lost Knight is an offline single-player game developed by Tuncay TÜRKYILMAZ. This policy explains how the game handles information.

Information we collect

Lost Knight does not collect, store on external servers, sell or share any personal data. The app does not request internet access and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or account SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example completed levels, items, language and sound settings) are saved only on your device, inside the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device through the game. If you have Android device backup turned on, Android may include this data in your own device backup; that backup is controlled by you and your Google account settings, not by the developer.

Deleting your data

You can delete all game data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Developer: Tuncay TÜRKYILMAZ. For questions about this policy, please use the developer contact email shown on the Lost Knight page in Google Play.
